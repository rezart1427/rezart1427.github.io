Research experience

  • 2026

    Stratification and shear in Long Island Sound

    Analyzed potential energy anomaly, stratification, and bulk shear from a multidecadal 3-D hindcast. Developed a block-balanced autoencoder and recurrent-state identification to examine physical-state transitions and their seasonal dependence.

  • 2026

    Hydrodynamics of Oyster Bay

    Developed a 3-D Delft3D-FM model of circulation, salinity, temperature, and bottom shear and examined their relationship with sediment and shellfish distributions.

  • 2025–2026

    Dissolved oxygen and hypoxia forecasting

    Developed and benchmarked tree-based and transformer-based models, Ridge autoregression, and persistence baselines for multi-horizon forecasts, evaluated using oxygen thresholds and state transitions.

  • 2025

    Coastal hypoxia recurrence

    Applied non-stationary generalized extreme-value models and return-period analysis across four U.S. coastal systems. Developed the Hypoxia Recurrence Risk Indicator (HRRI) to quantify recurrence, threshold sensitivity, and classification uncertainty.

  • 2024–2025

    Causal drivers of bottom-water oxygen

    Used PCMCI+ causal discovery, lagged conditional-independence testing, nonlinear forcing transformations, feature selection, OLS, and LASSO to identify physical and biogeochemical controls in Long Island Sound.

  • 2023–2024

    Lake Turkana circulation

    Used a 3-D ROMS model to investigate circulation and water-column dynamics.

  • 2018–2020

    Coastal modeling and marine energy

    Modeled tides and currents in Musa Bay to evaluate tidal-lagoon energy; used SWAN to examine wave-energy resources under climate scenarios; and simulated the 2018 Arvand River flood and its plume in the northern Persian Gulf.
