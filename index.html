Physical oceanography · Coastal science

Hi, I’m Reza Badpa.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Physical Oceanography at Stony Brook University’s School of Marine and Atmospheric Sciences in Stony Brook, NY.

My research examines how coastal circulation, stratification, and mixing shape dissolved oxygen and hypoxia in estuaries. I combine physical oceanography, hydrodynamic modeling, and machine learning to understand these processes and improve coastal oxygen predictions. We also developed the Hypoxia Recurrence Risk Indicator (HRRI) to track how often low-oxygen conditions recur across coastal ecosystems, accounting for sensitivity to oxygen thresholds and uncertainty in risk classification.

Much of my current work focuses on Long Island Sound, from identifying the physical and biogeochemical drivers of bottom-water oxygen to developing forecasts that capture the onset and recovery of hypoxia. I also study how low-oxygen conditions recur across U.S. coastal ecosystems.

Feel free to shoot me an email at reza.badpa@stonybrook.edu.

Research interests

Coastal dynamics and mixing

Stratification, bulk shear, and circulation in estuaries, using hydrodynamic simulations and observations to examine changes in the water column.

Dissolved oxygen and hypoxia

Causal discovery, extreme-value analysis, and recurrence indicators to understand the drivers and spatial patterns of low-oxygen conditions.

Physics-guided machine learning

Neural networks, autoencoders, and forecasting models that connect coastal physical processes with predictions of oxygen and state transitions.
